# First-session research synthesis

## Problem

We have anecdotal evidence that new admins struggle on the empty
dashboard, but no consolidated picture of *what* trips them up. This
synthesis pulls together support ticket analysis, the eight 1:1
interviews from February, and the bounce funnel data.

## Users

New workspace admins in their first session. Sample: 8 interviewees plus
2,140 sessions of behavioral data from January–February.

## Goals & Non-goals

**Goals:**
- Identify the top three tasks new admins try to do first.
- Identify the top three points of confusion.

**Non-goals:**
- Recommending a specific UI solution — that's downstream design work.
- Onboarding research for invited (non-admin) users.

## Flow

1. Reviewed all 312 support tickets tagged "new admin" from Q1.
2. Conducted 8 semi-structured interviews (45 min each).
3. Pulled behavioral funnel data for the first 5 minutes post-signup.
4. Open-coded interview transcripts; clustered into themes.
5. Cross-referenced themes against ticket frequency.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Top three first-session tasks identified with frequency.
- [x] Top three confusion points identified with example quotes.
- [x] Funnel data merged into the synthesis as a second axis.

## Accessibility

Not directly applicable to this research synthesis. Findings will be
applied to downstream specs (e.g. SPEC-2026-001) which carry their own
a11y requirements.

## Open questions

- Did interviewees self-select for being more frustrated? Recruited via
  the "having trouble?" Intercom prompt — yes, likely some bias.
