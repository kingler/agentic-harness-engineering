# Onboarding checklist on the empty dashboard

## Problem

New admins land on a blank dashboard and bounce within 90 seconds at a
38% rate (last quarter, n=2,140). Support tickets show three repeat
questions: "where do I add my first project?", "how do I invite my team?",
and "where is billing?". The dashboard does not surface any of these.

## Users

New workspace admins in their first session — typically the person who
signed up, before they invite teammates. Returning admins are out of scope.

## Goals & Non-goals

**Goals:**
- Reduce first-session bounce on the empty dashboard from 38% to below 25%.
- Surface the three top first-session tasks within the first scroll.
- Give admins a sense of progress as they complete tasks.

**Non-goals:**
- Onboarding for invited (non-admin) users.
- Persistent checklist that follows the user forever — the checklist
  disappears once all items are done.

## Flow

1. Admin lands on the dashboard with no projects, no teammates, and no
   billing set up.
2. A `Card` at the top of the dashboard shows a `Stepper` with three
   steps: *Create your first project*, *Invite a teammate*, *Add billing*.
3. Each step has a primary `Button` that takes the admin directly to the
   relevant page.
4. As steps complete, the `Stepper` updates and a thin `Banner` at the top
   of the card congratulates them.
5. When all three are complete, the entire `Card` collapses to a
   one-line summary and disappears on the next page load.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The checklist renders only when the workspace has zero projects,
      zero teammates, and no payment method.
- [ ] Each step's primary action navigates to the correct page within
      300ms (no intermediate confirmation).
- [ ] Completed steps update without a full page refresh.
- [ ] The checklist auto-dismisses on the next session after all three
      steps complete.
- [ ] The card is keyboard-reachable as the first focusable element on
      the dashboard.

## Accessibility

- The `Stepper` exposes step state via `aria-current="step"` on the active
  step.
- All step buttons have visible focus rings meeting 3:1 contrast.
- Step completion announces via an `aria-live="polite"` region: "Step 1
  of 3 complete: project created."
- Hit targets on all step buttons are at least 44×44 CSS pixels.
- Reduced motion: the step-complete animation respects
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Open questions

- Should we show the checklist again if the admin removes all projects
  later? (Likely no, but research is open.)
- Do we A/B test step ordering, or assume project-first based on
  research from SPEC-2026-004?
