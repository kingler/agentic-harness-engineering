Breakout 4 · ~12 min

Hooks & rules

Pair numbered rules (already in your system prompt) with deterministic enforcement — typically hooks/pre-tool-use.sh exiting non-zero with a reason the model must respect.

Goal

  • Three rules labeled R-01… in markdown if not already present.
  • One hook that blocks at least one dangerous tool/path/state tied to your top “never.”

Tips

  • Copy shell structure from exercises/sample-harness-uxd-specs/hooks/pre-tool-use.sh.
  • Log to stderr for humans; stdout/json may feed the agent depending on harness.
  • Keep guards narrow — blocking everything creates noisy false positives.
Soft vs hard Rules in prose remind the model; hooks and allow/deny lists make violations impossible. Design both — presenters called this component “Hooks & Rules” on purpose.
