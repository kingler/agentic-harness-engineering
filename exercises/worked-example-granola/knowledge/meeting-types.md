# Meeting types & glossary — Granola

> The recap rubric the `summarize-meeting` skill applies, plus the org glossary
> the agent must use. Stable domain facts; no secrets.

## Recap rubric by meeting type

| Type | Recap shape | Always capture | Never |
|------|-------------|----------------|-------|
| **Sales call** | Summary · open questions · next steps | Stage, timeline, blockers, who owns follow-up | Quote a price/term as agreed unless explicitly confirmed |
| **Standup** | Per-person: done / next / blockers | Blockers and their owner | Editorialize on velocity |
| **1:1** | Themes · agreements · follow-ups | Commitments both sides made | Record sensitive personal detail beyond the follow-up |
| **Interview** | Signal per competency · open questions | Concrete examples cited | A hire/no-hire recommendation (humans decide) |

## Uncertainty handling

If something was discussed but not confirmed (a price, a date, an owner), write
it under **⚠ Flagged — not confirmed** rather than asserting it. "Discussed" ≠
"agreed."

## Glossary / acronyms

- **Pro tier** — the paid plan; do not conflate with "Premium" (legacy name).
- **Security review** — the SOC2 questionnaire step, owned by the customer.
- **SE** — sales engineer (internal), not "software engineer".
