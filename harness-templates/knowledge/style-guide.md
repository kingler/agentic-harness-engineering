## Voice

- Sentence case in headings. No title case.
- Plain language. Reading age 12.
- No "please" before instructions. "Click Save" not "Please click Save".

## Numbers and units

- Use SI units. Never mix.
- Percentages with a percent sign, no space: `20%`.
- Money with currency symbol then digits: `$1,200`. Always include
  thousands separators above 999.

## Error copy

- Error messages tell the user what to do next, not just what went wrong.
- Don't blame the user. "We couldn't save your draft" not "You did X wrong".
- Cap at 12 words.

## Forbidden words

- "leverage" — say "use".
- "utilize" — say "use".
- "delight" — describe the actual outcome.
- "seamless" — say what's seamless about it.
